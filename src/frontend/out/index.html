Prediction. Proof. Planning.

Three pillars that no competing free or paid tool delivers together.

🔮

Predict, don't describe

Every existing bloom tool shows blooms that already formed. BloomCast fuses Sentinel-2 red-edge chlorophyll indices, 14-day Open-Meteo forecasts, and validated citizen observations into a calibrated 3–7 day forecast.

🎬

Bloom Replay Theatre

Scrub through documented historical bloom events and watch the forecast probability climb day-by-day before satellite confirmation. Proves the lead time is real.

🛡️

Resilience Sandbox

What-if climate and nutrient sliders produce per-waterbody projected annual high-risk days. Evidence for capital requests, stormwater planning, and policy decisions.

📊

Forecast Integrity Scorecard

Published Brier score, reliability diagram, hit/miss rates, and comparison vs. climatology and persistence baselines. Honest small-sample caveats included.

🌊

StreamFlush Nowcast

A second risk engine for narrow urban streams Sentinel-2 cannot resolve. Uses Open-Meteo only — rainfall × dry days × impervious surface → post-storm wash-off risk.

🩺

FHIR R4 One Health Alerts

Every alert exportable as a standards-compliant FHIR bundle. Speaks digital-health interoperability — for drinking water utilities, public health officers, and hospital networks.
